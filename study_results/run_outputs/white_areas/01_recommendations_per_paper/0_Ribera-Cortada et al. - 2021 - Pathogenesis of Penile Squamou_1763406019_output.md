Based on the provided paper, "Pathogenesis of Penile Squamous Cell Carcinoma: Molecular Update and Systematic Review," the following treatment recommendations, which are primarily potential targeted therapies based on molecular findings, have been identified.

The paper is a review of the molecular landscape of PSCC and does not provide recommendations based on clinical T-stages, tumor grade, or specific anatomical locations. All extracted recommendations are for systemic therapy and are contingent on the specific genetic profile of the tumor.

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy)** | For patients with PSCC harboring **amplifications of *EGFR* or cell cycle kinase *CDK4*, or somatic mutations in *KRAS***, the paper proposes targeting these specific molecular alterations. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy)** | For patients with PSCC and **concomitant *KRAS* and *PIK3CA* mutations**, it is suggested they might benefit from a combination of **mTOR and tyrosine-kinase inhibitors**. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy)** | For patients with **PDGFA-mutated tumors**, the use of **imatinib** is proposed. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy)** | For patients with ***TP53*-mutated tumors**, the use of **mTOR inhibitors** is suggested due to the interaction between mutant p53 and the PI3K signaling pathway. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy)** | For patients (particularly those with HPV-positive tumors) with **alterations in *ADAM6***, **EGFR**, or **COX2**, the paper proposes using **EGFR and COX2 inhibitors** and targeting *ADAM6* alterations. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Immunotherapy)** | For patients with **APOBEC-enriched tumors**, it is hypothesized that they might benefit from **immune checkpoint inhibitors**. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Targeted Therapy / Immunotherapy)** | For patients with a **defective DNA repair system and/or microsatellite instability**, treatment with **PARP inhibitors** (alone or combined with immune checkpoint inhibitors) is suggested. |
| **Clinical Trial Enrollment** | For patients with **NOTCH1-mutated tumors**, the paper suggests there is a rationale to enroll them in **clinical trials focused on PI3K/mTOR inhibitors**. |
