Based on the provided systematic review, the following explicit treatment recommendations are identified. It is important to note that this paper is a review of functional outcomes and not a clinical practice guideline; therefore, it contains a limited number of direct recommendations and does not provide comprehensive details on all aspects of care (e.g., follow-up schedules, systemic therapy).

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Treatment (Guiding Principle)** | **Organ-Sparing Surgery (OSS):** Organ-sparing surgery is recommended as a primary goal in the treatment of penile cancer whenever it is clinically feasible. This approach aims to preserve organ form and function. |
| **Primary Treatment (Surgical Technique)** | **Surgical Margins:** A conservative surgical approach with excision margins in the range of a few millimeters is considered acceptable. This is a change from the historically required 2-cm margin. |
| **Primary Treatment (Specific Modality)** | **Total Glans Resurfacing:** This is a potential treatment option for noninvasive tumors located on the glans, especially in cases where the tumor is diffuse. |
