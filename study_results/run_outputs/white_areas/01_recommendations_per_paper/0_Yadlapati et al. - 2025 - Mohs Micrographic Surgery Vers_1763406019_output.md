Based on the provided systematic review, here are the explicit treatment recommendations for penile squamous cell carcinoma (SCC).

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Treatment** | For **early-stage penile SCC**, treatment options include Wide Local Excision (WLE), Mohs Micrographic Surgery (MMS), glansectomy, or partial penectomy. |
| **Primary Treatment** | For more **advanced cases of penile SCC**, a total penectomy is needed. |
| **Primary Treatment** | When performing Wide Local Excision (WLE) for **penile SCC**, a **1 cm margin** is often suggested to reduce recurrence. |
| **Primary Treatment** | For **penile SCC**, Mohs Micrographic Surgery (MMS) should be considered a viable and appropriate option as it is associated with a **statistically significant lower local recurrence rate** (5.4% for MMS vs. 16.8% for WLE) compared to Wide Local Excision (WLE). MMS also offers potential for improved quality of life through more precise tissue preservation and decreased scarring. |
