Based on the systematic review provided, the following explicit treatment recommendations are extracted and organized as requested.

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Tumor Treatment** | **Recommendation:** Radiation treatment is an alternative organ-preserving approach for primary tumors.<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• **Stage:** T1-2 lesions<br>• **Size:** < 4 cm in diameter<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• Radiation therapy is presented as an alternative to surgery.<br><br>**Additional Clinical Clarifications:**<br>• This approach has the advantage of preserving sexual function, especially in younger patients. |
| **Lymph Node Management (Adjuvant Therapy)** | **Recommendation:** Adjuvant Radiotherapy (RT) following Pelvic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND) is **not recommended** as a routine, standard clinical practice.<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• Node-positive penile cancer patients (specifically discussed in the context of pN3).<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• The use of adjuvant RT after PLND is not recommended due to insufficient evidence and the low quality of available data.<br><br>**Additional Clinical Clarifications:**<br>• The authors conclude that the efficacy of this approach for locoregional control and survival is inconclusive. |
| **Lymph Node Management (Adjuvant Therapy)** | **Recommendation:** The use of Adjuvant Radiotherapy (RT) after Pelvic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND) should be limited.<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• Node-positive penile cancer patients.<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• Administration of adjuvant RT after PLND should be restricted to the context of **prospective controlled clinical trials only**, until further high-quality evidence becomes available. |
| **Lymph Node Management (Surgical)** | **Recommendation:** The necessity of Pelvic Lymph Node Dissection (PLND) is stage-dependent, based on the findings reported from a reviewed study (Lont et al.).<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• **Unnecessary:** for pathological stages pN0 and pN1.<br>• **Should be performed:** for pathological stage pN2.<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• For pN2 and pN3 stages, the reviewed study suggested that PLND alone or combined with adjuvant RT was not sufficient for disease control. |
