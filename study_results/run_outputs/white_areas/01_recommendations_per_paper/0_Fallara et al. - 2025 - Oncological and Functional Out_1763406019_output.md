Based on the systematic review provided, the following treatment recommendations and indications for Penile Shaft Sparing (PSS) surgery are identified. The paper primarily summarizes existing evidence rather than making new, definitive recommendations, a limitation it explicitly states due to the low quality of the reviewed studies.

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Treatment (Penile Shaft Sparing Surgery)** | **Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• **Stage:** The indication for Penile Shaft Sparing (PSS) surgery is usually restricted to localised Penile Cancer (PeCa) stages **pTis, pTa, and pT1**.<br>• **Anatomical Specifics:** Circumcision is specifically indicated for localised PeCa that is **limited to the foreskin**.<br><br>**Treatment Modalities and Conditions:**<br>The following techniques are described as viable PSS options for the indicated stages:<br>• **Circumcision**<br>• **Wide Local Excision (WLE)**, with or without a split skin graft (SSG)<br>• **Laser Ablation** (e.g., CO₂, Nd:YAG)<br>• **Glans Resurfacing**<br>• **Glansectomy**, with or without a split skin graft (SSG)<br><br>**Additional Clinical Clarifications / Limitations:**<br>• The review notes that while these techniques are all viable options, there is no evidence or precise recommendation available in international guidelines to guide the specific choice of one technique over another in different clinical settings.<br>• The authors conclude that due to the overall low quality of the available data and the absence of comparative studies, **definitive recommendations cannot be drawn** regarding the safety and efficacy of PSS for localised PeCa. |
