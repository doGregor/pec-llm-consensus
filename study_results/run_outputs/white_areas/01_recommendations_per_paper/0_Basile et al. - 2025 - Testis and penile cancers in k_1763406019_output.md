Based on the systematic review provided, the following explicit treatment recommendations for penile cancer in kidney transplant recipients are identified:

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Screening** | General screening protocols for penile cancer are not recommended in kidney transplant recipients. |
| **Primary Treatment (Localized Disease)** | For localized penile cancer, the surgical treatment of the primary tumor should be consistent with the established guidelines for the general population. Deviation from current treatment guidelines is not recommended for these cases. |
| **Lymph Node Management** | The management of inguinal nodal disease does not pose additional risks for transplanted patients with penile cancer, and therefore should be consistent with standard oncological guidelines. |
| **Treatment (Locally Advanced Disease)** | For patients with locally advanced disease, treatment should be considered on an individualized basis with the primary goal of minimizing the risk of graft injury. Any non-cytotoxic treatment option should be preferred over radiotherapy and cytotoxic treatments. |
