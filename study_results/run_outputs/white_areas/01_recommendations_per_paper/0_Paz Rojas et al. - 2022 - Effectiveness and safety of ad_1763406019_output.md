Based on the provided systematic review and meta-analysis, the following explicit treatment recommendations and key conclusions regarding the management of penile cancer are extracted:

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Systemic Therapy (Adjuvant)** | Adjuvant Chemotherapy (AC) is indicated in patients with palpable, multiple, or bilateral lymph nodes fixed to deep planes (N+ according to the cTNM classification). It is also specifically indicated for patients with pathologically confirmed pN2-3 disease. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Neoadjuvant)** | Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy (NC) is indicated and currently mostly reserved for patients with clinically advanced nodal disease (cN3). Subsequent loco-regional surgical management is then decided based on the macroscopic lymph node response. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Neoadjuvant)** | Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy (NC) can be utilized when initial local control is desired, with the specific goal of enabling a subsequent, less aggressive radical local surgery. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Comparison)** | For node-positive patients, Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy (NC) offers a significant **overall survival** benefit when compared to receiving no intervention. |
| **Systemic Therapy (Comparison)** | For node-positive patients, Adjuvant Chemotherapy (AC) results in a better **progression-free survival** response when compared to Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy (NC). |
