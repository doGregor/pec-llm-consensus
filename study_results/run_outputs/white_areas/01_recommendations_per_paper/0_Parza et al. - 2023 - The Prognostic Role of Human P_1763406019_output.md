Based on a thorough review of the provided systematic review, the following explicit treatment and diagnostic recommendations were identified. The paper's primary focus is on the prognostic role of HPV/p16, so it primarily cites recommendations from other guidelines regarding diagnostic workup rather than detailing specific treatment modalities.

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Diagnosis / Staging** | **EAU-ASCO Guideline for Biomarker Testing:** It is recommended to conduct p16 testing through Immunohistochemistry (IHC) in all individuals diagnosed with Penile Squamous Cell Carcinoma (PSCC). Testing for HPV status in the initial diagnostic workup carries a level 2a evidence grade. |
| **Diagnosis / Classification** | **WHO 2022 Recommendation:** It is recommended to utilize p16 screening whenever possible to differentiate between HPV-associated and HPV-independent PSCC. |
| **Primary Treatment Strategy** | **Localized PSCC:** Surgery can be curative in localized cases. |
| **Treatment for Advanced Disease** | **Locally Advanced and Advanced PSCC:** A multimodal therapeutic approach is typically warranted for patients with lymph node involvement. |
