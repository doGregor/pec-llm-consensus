Based on the systematic review provided, the following explicit treatment recommendations for Penile Intraepithelial Neoplasia (PeIN) have been extracted and organized as requested.

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Treatment** | **For PeIN of the Prepuce (Foreskin):**<br>• **Specific Clinical Indication:** Penile Intraepithelial Neoplasia (PeIN) located on the prepuce.<br>• **Treatment Modality:** Circumcision is the preferred treatment.<br>• **Additional Clinical Clarifications:** The paper notes that circumcision cleared all preputial PeIN in the reviewed studies and may also decrease the toxicity associated with topical treatment of the glans. |
| **Primary Treatment** | **For PeIN of the Glans (First-line):**<br>• **Specific Clinical Indication:** Penile Intraepithelial Neoplasia (PeIN) located on the glans.<br>• **Treatment Modality:** Organ-sparing approaches, including topical treatments (e.g., imiquimod, 5-fluorouracil) or laser treatments, should be recommended as first-line options.<br>• **Additional Clinical Clarifications:** This recommendation is based on their efficacy and relatively low rates of recurrence and progression. |
| **Primary Treatment** | **For PeIN of the Glans (Second-line after failure of first-line):**<br>• **Specific Clinical Indication:** Penile Intraepithelial Neoplasia (PeIN) of the glans after failure of a first-line topical or laser treatment.<br>• **Treatment Modality:** Patients should be offered a second topical or laser treatment.<br>• **Indications of treatment limitations:** The paper explicitly states that data describing the efficacy of further lines of treatment after first-line failure are limited. |
| **Primary Treatment** | **For Extensive or Recurrent PeIN of the Glans (Third-line):**<br>• **Specific Clinical Indication:** Extensive PeIN or recurrent PeIN after failure of both first- and second-line topical and/or laser treatments.<br>• **Treatment Modality:** Surgical options, including wide local excision or glans resurfacing, should be offered. |
| **Follow-up Care** | **For all men treated for PeIN:**<br>• **Specific Clinical Indication:** Applies to all patients after any curative-intent treatment for PeIN.<br>• **Treatment Modality:** Close follow-up is essential.<br>• **Additional Clinical Clarifications:** Follow-up is necessary due to the considerable risk of recurrence for all treatment options and the potential for progression to invasive penile cancer. The paper does not specify the frequency or duration of follow-up. |
