Based on the systematic review provided, the following explicit treatment recommendations for localised penile cancer are identified:

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **General Treatment Principle** | **Penile-preserving surgery should be considered in all patients with localised Penile Cancer (PeCa).** This approach aims to maintain sexual and urinary function without compromising oncological outcomes. |
| **Primary Treatment** | For **localised PeCa confined to the glans**, available penile-preserving treatments include **laser therapy, glans resurfacing, or glansectomy**. The specific treatment choice depends on the disease stage. |
| **Primary Treatment** | For **clinical tumour stage 2 (cT2) PeCa**, **glansectomy is an option**. <br> **Additional Clinical Clarification:** If there is doubt about cavernosa involvement, an MRI should be performed to exclude involvement of the distal corpus cavernosa (which would indicate cT3 disease). |
| **Primary Treatment** | For **invasive cT2 PeCa**, **glansectomy is the widely accepted procedure of choice.** |
| **Surgical Management (Intraoperative)** | If, during glansectomy, the **distal corporal bodies are found to be macroscopically involved**, **distal corporectomies are performed.** |
| **Reconstructive Surgery** | For patients undergoing glansectomy who **desire a neoglans**, a **split-thickness skin graft (SSG) can be used for reconstruction.** |
