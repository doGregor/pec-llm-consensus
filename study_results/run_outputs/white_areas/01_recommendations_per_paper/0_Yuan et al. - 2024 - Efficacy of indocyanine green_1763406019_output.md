Based on the research paper provided, the following explicit treatment recommendations for penile cancer are identified and extracted:

| Aspect of Care | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lymph Node Management / Surgical Staging** | **Accurate surgical staging of inguinal lymph nodes (ILNs) is recommended** for comprehensive evaluation and treatment.<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• Selected patients with penile cancer, specifically those with:<br>  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• T-stage T1b-T4 and clinically non-palpable nodes (**N0**).<br>  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Any T-stage with clinically palpable nodes (**N1–3**).<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• Modalities include video-endoscopic or open modified inguinal lymph node dissection (ILND). |
| **Lymph Node Management / Surgical Staging (Alternative)** | **Dynamic sentinel lymph node (LN) sampling may also be recommended** for the surgical staging of ILNs.<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• Clinically LN-negative patients.<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• Dynamic sentinel LN sampling.<br><br>**Additional Clinical Clarifications:**<br>• This recommendation is qualified with the clarification that concerns about **high false-negative rates and limited technique availability** in some centers should be emphasized when considering this approach. |
| **Lymph Node Management / Surgical Technique** | **Indocyanine green (ICG) fluorescence-guided inguinal lymph node dissection (ILND) is beneficial and recommended.**<br><br>**Specific Clinical Indications:**<br>• Selected patients with penile cancer, as defined by the study's inclusion criteria:<br>  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• **Stage-specific details:** Squamous cell carcinoma, pathological stage **pT1b–pT4**.<br>  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;• Patients with an established indication for ILND.<br><br>**Treatment Modality with Precise Conditions:**<br>• Indocyanine green (ICG) fluorescence-guided ILND, performed via a laparoscopic or robot-assisted approach.<br><br>**Additional Clinical Clarifications:**<br>• This technique is recommended specifically for patients with **clinically non-palpable nodes (cN0M0)** undergoing staging ILND. It is endorsed because it improves the number of retrieved ILNs and reduces the LN non-compliance rate without increasing surgical complications. |
